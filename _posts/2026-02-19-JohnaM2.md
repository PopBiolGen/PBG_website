PBG PhD student John recently achieved his PhD Milestone 2! As part of his Milestone 2 requirements, John delivered a presentation on the work he has undertaken to date, and planned work for the remainder of his PhD project ‘Modelling the spread and control of a malaria vector’.
The goal of Milestone 2 is for PhD candidates to reflect on the progress made in their research projects and to finalise a strategy for the timely completion and dissemination of their research findings.
Congratulations John!
