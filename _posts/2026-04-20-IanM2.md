PBG PhD student Ian recently achieved his PhD Milestone 2! As part of his Milestone 2 requirements, Ian delivered a presentation on the work he has undertaken to date, and planned work for the remainder of his PhD project ‘Interrogating the family: Estimating fine-scale dispersal distance of invasive species in WA by linking kinship and resistance kernels’.
The goal of Milestone 2 is for PhD candidates to reflect on the progress made in their research projects and to finalise a strategy for the timely completion and dissemination of their research findings.
Congratulations Ian!
