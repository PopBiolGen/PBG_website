PBG PhD student Normandie recently achieved her PhD Milestone 2! As part of her Milestone 2 requirements, Normandie delivered a presentation on the work she has undertaken to date, and planned work for the remainder of her PhD project ‘Burning Bridges: Early eradication and management of invasive species’.
The goal of Milestone 2 is for PhD candidates to reflect on the progress made in their research projects and to finalise a strategy for the timely completion and dissemination of their research findings.
Congratulations Normandie!
