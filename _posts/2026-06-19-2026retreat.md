In June, the Population Biology and Genomics team embarked on our 2026 Team Retreat. We spent five-days on the tranquil Moonrise Farm in Margaret River, where we spent time working on writing, team building, and undertaking targeted workshops. Ben facilitated a multi-part ‘career planning’ workshop and Brenton ran a workshop on the use of AI.
We also took the opportunity to have some fun – we took a walk along the Busselton Jetty, visited Lake Cave, visited the resident farm animals, explored the surrounding bushland, went bird watching, and played some games.
