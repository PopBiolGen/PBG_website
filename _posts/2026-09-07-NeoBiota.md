PBG PhD students Ian and Normandie recently attended NeoBiota 2026: 14th International Conference on Biological Invasions in Brussels, Belgium. The week-long conference brought together 371 attendees from 45 nationalities. 
Normandie presented a talk titled ‘The Eleventh Hour: Defining early intervention for the eradication of invasive species’ where she outlined results from the first chapter of her PhD . 

Ian presented a short talk and ePoster titled 'Tracking the killer's kin: Estimating natal dispersal of feral cats in southwestern Australia' showcasing some of his PhD results. Ian’s poster was well received, being awarded the Best Poster Award. Congratulations, Ian!
