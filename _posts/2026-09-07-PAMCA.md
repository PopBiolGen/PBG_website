PBG PhD student John recently attended the 2026 Pan-African Mosquito Control Association (PAMCA) Conference in Nairobi, Kenya. The conference brought together leading experts in the field of malaria vector control from the research, industry, policy, and health sectors. 
Held over three days, the conference showcased cutting-edge science and collaborations, and provided the opportunity for networking, and discussions around the best practices, advances and challenges of mosquito control in Africa. 
John presented a talk titled ‘Genetic allee effect: exploiting the fate of small populations to contain invasive vectors’, outlining some of his PhD research.
