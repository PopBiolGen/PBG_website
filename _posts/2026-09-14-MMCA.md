PBG PhD student John recently attended the 2026 Malaria Modellers Conference Australia (MMCA2026) in Perth.
John presented a poster titled ‘Controlling the spread of the invasive urban malaria vector’ which showcased some of his PhD research.
The MMCA2026 conference brought together researchers working in malaria modelling to share their research, exchange ideas, and build professional networks. The conference was a great opportunity for early-career malaria modellers to connect, foster collaborations, and expand their networks.
