Jasper is a Research Officer working on the Toad Containment Zone project, where he supports research activities related to fauna and toad monitoring with Indigenous Ranger groups, pastoralists and scientists. He assists with data collection and analysis, fieldwork, and the development of research outputs and reports. Before his current role, Jasper spent three years working as a Ranger Coordinator in a remote Aboriginal community in Western Australia. Prior to that, he worked for three years as a technology consultant across two multinational professional services firms. Jasper has a key focus on bringing together a wide range of stakeholders through communication in order to achieve shared goals.
